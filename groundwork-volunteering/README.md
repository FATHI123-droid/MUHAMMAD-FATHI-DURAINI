# Groundwork & Volunteering

### Practical Engagement · Field-Based Learning · Humanitarian Affairs

This section documents practical engagement, volunteering, training and field-based learning connected to **human rights, humanitarian affairs, community engagement, youth development and international cooperation**.

The purpose is to connect academic learning with practical experiences and to reflect on how international relations and human rights issues are encountered beyond the classroom.

# WHY GROUNDWORK MATTERS

Academic knowledge provides frameworks for understanding international and domestic issues.

Practical engagement provides an opportunity to observe how those issues affect institutions, communities and individuals.

This section therefore treats experience as part of the learning process:

**Learn → Engage → Reflect → Improve**

---

# DOCUMENTATION PRINCIPLES

### Accuracy

Experiences are documented based on actual participation and responsibilities.

### Reflection

The focus is on what was learned, not simply on listing activities.

### Evidence

Where appropriate, supporting documents, certificates, reports or official programme materials may be included.

### Integrity

Roles and achievements are presented accurately without overstating responsibilities or outcomes.
