# 02 · INTERNATIONAL HUMAN RIGHTS INSTRUMENTS

## Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC)

**Adopted:** 1989
**Relevance:** Provides the international legal framework concerning the rights of children.

**Malaysia:** Party

**Source:** United Nations Treaty Collection / OHCHR

---

## Convention on the Elimination of All Forms of Discrimination against Women (CEDAW)

**Adopted:** 1979
**Relevance:** Establishes international standards concerning the elimination of discrimination against women.

**Malaysia:** Party

**Source:** United Nations Treaty Collection / OHCHR

---

## Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD)

**Adopted:** 2006
**Relevance:** Establishes international standards concerning the rights and participation of persons with disabilities.

**Malaysia:** Party

**Source:** United Nations Treaty Collection / OHCHR

---
## Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)

**Instrument Type:** Declaration

**Adopted:** 1948
**Relevance:** Provides a foundational international human rights standard and reference point for the development and interpretation of international human rights norms.

**Malaysia:** UN Member State

**Source:** United Nations / OHCHR

## International Covenant on Economic, Social and Cultural Rights (ICESCR)

**Adopted:** 1966

# 03 · UNITED NATIONS MATERIALS

## OHCHR — Treaty Body Database

**Institution:** Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Human Rights

**Purpose:** To verify treaty status, reporting information and relevant human rights documentation concerning Malaysia.

**Use in research:** Treaty status, State reporting and international human rights mechanisms.

---

## CRC Treaty Body Materials — Malaysia

**Institution:** United Nations Committee on the Rights of the Child

**Relevance:** Records the Committee's review of Malaysia's implementation of the Convention on the Rights of the Child.

**Key materials:** Malaysia's replies to the List of Issues and the Committee's concluding observations following the examination of Malaysia's combined second to fourth periodic reports in 2026.

**Source:** OHCHR Treaty Body Database

---

## CRPD Treaty Body Materials — Malaysia

**Institution:** United Nations Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities

**Relevance:** Records treaty-body materials concerning Malaysia's implementation of the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.

**Research status:** Malaysia's initial State party report and the subsequent review process will be monitored through the OHCHR Treaty Body Database.

**Source:** OHCHR Treaty Body Database

---

## Universal Periodic Review — Malaysia

**Institution:** United Nations Human Rights Council

**Purpose:** Periodic review of the human rights situation in Malaysia.

**Use in research:** International recommendations, government responses and developments in Malaysia's engagement with the UN human rights system.

# SOURCE EVALUATION

Each source will be assessed according to:

| Criterion      | Question                                               |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Authority      | Who produced the source?                               |
| Primary Status | Is it an original legal or institutional source?       |
| Relevance      | Does it directly support the research question?        |
| Currency       | Is the information sufficiently current?               |
| Reliability    | Can the information be independently verified?         |
| Academic Value | Does it contribute to the legal or scholarly analysis? |

# RESEARCH INTEGRITY

Sources will not be used merely to support a predetermined conclusion.

Where sources present different interpretations, the research will identify the relevant positions and examine the underlying legal or empirical basis.

Claims will be distinguished from:

* Established legal rules
* Institutional positions
* Academic interpretations
* Empirical evidence
* The author's own analysis
