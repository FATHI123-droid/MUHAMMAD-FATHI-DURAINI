Research note

This page presents the academic literature identified during the initial collection stage. It is not intended to represent an exhaustive systematic literature review.
01

Research Purpose

Academic literature provides the conceptual and analytical foundation for this research. The purpose of this collection is to identify how scholars have examined treaty implementation, domestic courts, international human rights law, federal treaty-making powers and national human rights institutions.

The literature is therefore used not simply as background reading, but as a means of identifying competing approaches, analytical concepts, unresolved questions and areas requiring further research in the Malaysian context.

02

Literature Map

The initial collection can be organised into six overlapping areas of inquiry.

01
Domestic Courts

How domestic courts interact with international human rights norms and treaties.

02
Treaty Application

The relationship between treaty obligations and domestic legal development.

03

05

Preliminary Finding

The literature points toward a layered relationship between international standards and domestic law.

The initial collection suggests that the central analytical problem cannot be reduced to whether international human rights law is simply “used” by domestic actors. The more precise questions concern treaty status, domestic incorporation, judicial interpretation, institutional practice and the role of international monitoring mechanisms.

This provides a basis for connecting the literature review with the constitutional, treaty, case-law and SUHAKAM materials examined elsewhere in Research Project 01.

06

Research Integrity

The literature collection is treated as an analytical resource rather than a source-selection exercise designed to confirm a predetermined conclusion.

Academic interpretations will be distinguished from primary legal rules, institutional positions and the author's own analysis. Where scholars adopt different approaches, the underlying reasoning and evidentiary basis should be examined rather than assuming that one interpretation is automatically authoritative.


Bibliographic details will be expanded and independently verified as the literature review develops.
