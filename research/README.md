# Research

### Original Academic Research & Analytical Writing

This section documents original academic research and analytical work developed through my study of **international relations, international law, human rights and humanitarian affairs**.

The purpose is to develop research that is evidence-based, legally and theoretically grounded, and relevant to contemporary issues.

---

## RESEARCH FOCUS

### International Law

Research examining international legal rules, principles, institutions and contemporary legal issues.

### Human Rights

Research concerning international human rights standards, protection mechanisms, rights-based approaches and human rights issues in Malaysia and beyond.

### International Humanitarian Law

Study of the legal framework applicable during armed conflict, including civilian protection, conduct of hostilities and humanitarian principles.

### International Relations

Analysis of international institutions, global governance, international cooperation, conflict, security and relations between states and other international actors.

### Humanitarian Affairs

Research concerning humanitarian action, vulnerable populations, humanitarian principles and the interaction between law, policy and humanitarian practice.

---

# RESEARCH APPROACH

Each research project follows a structured process:

**Research Question**
↓
**Primary Sources & Evidence**
↓
**Literature Review**
↓
**Theoretical / Legal Framework**
↓
**Analysis**
↓
**Findings & Implications**

Priority is given to **primary and authoritative sources**, including:

* International treaties and conventions
* International court and tribunal decisions
* United Nations documents
* National legislation and official government documents
* Reports from recognised international organisations
* Peer-reviewed academic literat
