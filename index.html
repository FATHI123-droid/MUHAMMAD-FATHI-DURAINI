Research Philosophy

What the archive
is built around.

Primary sources first.

Legal and institutional claims should be traceable to authoritative materials wherever possible.

Distinguish law from interpretation.

The archive separates established legal rules from institutional positions, academic arguments and independent analysis.

Research before conclusion.

Research questions are explored through evidence rather than built around a predetermined conclusion.

Keep the questions open.

Strong research does not only record what is known. It also identifies what remains uncertain or contested.
