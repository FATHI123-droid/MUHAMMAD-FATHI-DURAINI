03 · Research Method

How the work is built.

The portfolio is designed around a simple principle: claims should remain connected to sources, legal reasoning and clearly identified analytical limits.

01

Source

Begin with primary legal materials, official institutional documents, treaty texts, judicial decisions and authoritative international sources.

02

Context

Place each source within its legal, institutional, historical and international context before drawing analytical conclusions.

03

Analysis

Distinguish established legal rules from institutional positions, academic interpretations, empirical evidence and independent analysis.

04

Questions

Use findings to identify unresolved questions and directions for further research rather than forcing premature conclusions.

04 · Intellectual Map
